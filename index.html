Behind the work

I've seen great brands lose sales to stores that didn't match the product behind them. By understanding the brand, the customer and the business around it, I build stores that feel intuitive, considered and made to sell.

Slow pages, confusing carts, offers that don't land. With 50+ stores shipped last year, from custom Shopify themes to headless storefronts, every build starts with how the customer buys, and the design and code follow from there.

From theme architecture and front-end to subscriptions, bundles and the conversion work, I handle the whole build, so the technical side and the brand side line up and the store sells as well as it looks.

I'm comfortable leading projects from idea to launch, or joining an existing team and making things better. Based in UTC+8, working with brands worldwide.
